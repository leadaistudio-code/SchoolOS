## In-video checkpoints

- 0:52: Scores: great +2, food 0, was 0. What is “food was great”? (answer: +2, happy)
- 1:08: Why does “not good” fool a word-score model? (answer: Good adds points even though not flips the meaning)

[00:00:52.100] But look at not good. Not scores zero and good scores plus one, so the total is plus one: happy. Wrong! Simple word scores ignore word order, so not fools them.
[00:01:08.500] Sarcasm is even trickier. Oh great, cold samosas again sounds happy word by word, but it is not. Modern AI looks at whole sentences and word order, and learns from millions of examples, which is why it needs so much data.
[00:01:28.700] (Outro) Let us recap. Words become numbers for an AI. Adding word scores gives a sentiment. Not, and sarcasm, fool simple word scores. Next: How chatbots write: predicting the next word.

## In-video checkpoints

- 0:56: Which set of examples will train a smarter model? (answer: Shapes of different sizes, neat and messy)
- 1:33: Your model calls squares circles. What is the best fix? (answer: Add more varied squares and circles, then retrain)

# Video 5: Train your first AI model

[00:00:00.000] (Intro) MyCampusView Academy. AI for Young Builders, video 5. Train your first AI model.
[00:00:10.100] Time to build! In this lesson you will train an image classifier: an AI that sorts drawings into groups you choose.
[00:00:20.500] There are four steps. Choose your groups, like circle, triangle and square. Draw at least five examples of each. Press Train. Then draw something new and see what your model thinks.
[00:00:38.100] Here is the secret to a smart model: variety. Draw some shapes big and some small, some neat and some messy, in different places on the canvas. The more kinds of example it sees, the better it copes with new drawings.
[00:00:56.600] When you test a drawing, the model shows its confidence. Here it is sixty-seven percent sure this is a triangle. Low confidence means the drawing sits between groups, and your model needs more examples.
[00:01:13.200] If your model mixes two groups up, add more examples of both, especially the kind it got wrong, then train again. Test, find mistakes, add better data, retrain. That loop is exactly what AI engineers do every day.
[00:01:33.200] Your model trains right in your browser, so your drawings are not sent anywhere to train it. Big image AIs work the same way, just with millions of examples and much bigger models.
[00:01:48.100] (Outro) Let us recap. Choose groups and draw labelled examples. Press Train, then try new drawings. Low confidence means the model needs better examples. Next: How your AI decides: nearest neighbours.

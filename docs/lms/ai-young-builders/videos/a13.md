## In-video checkpoints

- 0:53: A shocking video of a famous person appears in your group chat. What first? (answer: Check where it came from before sharing)
- 1:35: Which is an honest way to use AI for homework, when allowed? (answer: Ask it to explain, then write your own answer and say you used AI)

## Narration script

Clear, friendly, slow Indian English for Classes 5–8. Pronounce: AI (A-I), k-NN (K-N-N), RGB (R-G-B), Bhashini (BHAA-shi-nee).

```text
# Video 13: Responsible AI: privacy, deepfakes and you

[00:00:00.000] (Intro) MyCampusView Academy. AI for Young Builders, video 13. Responsible AI: privacy, deepfakes and you.
[00:00:12.100] AI is a powerful tool. Like any tool, it can be used well or badly. The choice is yours.
[00:00:22.200] First, privacy. AI apps may keep what you type or upload. So share as little as possible, and never share other people’s photos or details without asking them.
[00:00:37.100] Second, truth. AI can make deepfakes: fake photos, videos and voices of real people. Shocking content is made to spread fast, so slow down before you believe it or share it.
[00:00:53.400] Use the word STOP. Source: who posted it first? Too surprising? Slow down. Odd details: strange hands, blurry edges, lips that do not match the words. Proof: can you find it on two trusted sites? If not, do not share.
[00:01:17.300] Third, honesty. Using AI for ideas or explanations is fine when your teacher allows it. Copying its answers and calling them your own is not. Write in your own words, and say when and how you used AI.
[00:01:35.500] (Outro) Let us recap. Share as little personal information as possible. STOP and check before you believe or share. Use AI honestly and never to hurt anyone. Next: Final project: build and present your AI.
```

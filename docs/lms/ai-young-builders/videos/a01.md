## In-video checkpoints

- 1:30: Which of these uses AI? (answer: A phone that unlocks when it sees your face)
- 2:10: A chatbot asks for your home address. What do you do? (answer: Do not share it, and tell an adult)

## Narration script

Clear, friendly, slow Indian English for Classes 5–8. Pronounce: AI (A-I), k-NN (K-N-N), RGB (R-G-B), Bhashini (BHAA-shi-nee).

```text
# Video 1: Welcome: AI is all around you

[00:00:00.000] (Intro) MyCampusView Academy. AI for Young Builders, video 1. Welcome: AI is all around you.
[00:00:10.800] Welcome to AI for Young Builders! In this course you will build your very own AI, one that learns to recognise drawings and things you show it, right in your browser.
[00:00:24.200] You probably used AI today without noticing. Your phone unlocks when it sees your face. A maps app predicts traffic. An app translates Hindi into Tamil. A video app suggests what to watch next. Every one of these learned patterns from examples.
[00:00:47.200] So what is AI? Artificial intelligence means a computer doing something we usually think needs a human brain. Most AI does this by learning from lots of examples. It is not magic, and it is not alive. It is a pattern-finder, built by people.
[00:01:09.200] AI is busy in India too. Banks use it to spot suspicious UPI payments in a split second. Bhashini, a Government of India project, translates between Indian languages. And farmers can photograph a leaf to check for crop disease.
[00:01:30.100] Here is your journey. You will train your own image classifier. You will test it and fix its mistakes. You will talk to a real AI chatbot, safely. And you will learn how to make AI fairer for everyone.
[00:01:49.300] Before we start, five Safe AI rules. Keep personal details private. Remember that AI can be wrong, so check important facts. Be kind. Be honest when you use AI. And ask an adult if anything worries you.
[00:02:10.800] (Outro) Let us recap. AI is a computer doing tasks that usually need human thinking. Most AI learns patterns from examples. AI can be wrong, so keep personal details private and check facts. Next: Rules or learning? How AI learns.
```

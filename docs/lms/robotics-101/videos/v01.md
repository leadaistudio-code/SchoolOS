## Narration script

Clear, friendly, slow Indian English for Classes 5–8. Pronounce: Arduino (aar-DWEE-no), L298N (L-two-nine-eight-N), PWM (P-W-M), infrared (in-fra-red).

```text
# Video 1: Welcome to Robotics 101

[00:00:00.000] (Intro) MyCampusView Academy. Robotics 101. Welcome to Robotics 101.
[00:00:04.500] Hello and welcome! In this course you will build a small robot that can follow a black line on the floor, all by itself.
[00:00:17.500] Here is the robot you will build, shown in our simulator. It has two eyes underneath that look at the floor. When the line bends, the robot turns to follow it. Nobody is steering it. The robot decides on its own.
[00:00:40.000] Robots like this are used in real life. Warehouse robots follow lines to carry parcels. Some hospitals use them to deliver medicines, and factories use them to move parts from one machine to the next.
[00:00:58.000] The course has four modules. First, you meet your robot. Next, you learn how its parts work. Then you write the program. Finally, you build it, test it and make it better.
[00:01:14.500] Every lesson follows the same pattern. You watch a short video, try an activity or the simulator, check what you learned with a few questions, and then build and test.
[00:01:30.500] Do not worry if you do not have a robot kit yet. Every lesson works in your browser, and the simulator lets you test the same ideas. You can even complete the final challenge on the virtual pathway.
[00:01:50.000] Safety first. Always ask an adult before you use batteries. Never let the battery wires touch each other. Unplug the power before you change any wires, and keep your fingers away from the wheels when the motors are running.
[00:02:10.000] By the end, you will have a working robot, a certificate, and six badges to collect along the way. Let us begin!
[00:02:22.000] (Outro) Next: Understanding robots: sense, think, act.
```

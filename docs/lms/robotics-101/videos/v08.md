## Narration script

Clear, friendly, slow Indian English for Classes 5–8. Pronounce: Arduino (aar-DWEE-no), L298N (L-two-nine-eight-N), PWM (P-W-M), infrared (in-fra-red).

```text
# Video 8: Your first Arduino program

[00:00:00.000] (Intro) MyCampusView Academy. Robotics 101, video 8. Your first Arduino program.
[00:00:10.100] Now you will write your first program. Programs for Arduino are called sketches. You write them in the Arduino IDE, which is free.
[00:00:22.400] Install the Arduino IDE and plug in the board with the USB cable. Under Tools, choose the board Arduino Uno, and then choose the port your Arduino is on.
[00:00:35.600] Every sketch has two parts. setup runs once, when the robot switches on. loop runs again and again, forever. Remember sense, think, act? That happens inside loop.
[00:00:52.900] Here is a tiny program. pinMode makes pin 13 an output. In the loop, we switch the light on, wait half a second, switch it off, and wait again. Every instruction ends with a semicolon.
[00:01:10.600] Click the tick to check your code. If you see a red error, read it carefully: it is often a missing semicolon or bracket. Then click the arrow to upload, and watch the light blink.
[00:01:25.600] (Outro) Let us recap. setup() runs once; loop() runs forever. Every instruction ends with a semicolon. Verify, then upload. Next: Reading the IR sensors in code.
```

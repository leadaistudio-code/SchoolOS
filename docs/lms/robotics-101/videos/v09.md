## In-video checkpoints

- 1:04: Your sensor is over black tape but the Serial Monitor says WHITE. What is the simplest fix? (answer: Change ON_LINE to LOW)

## Narration script

Clear, friendly, slow Indian English for Classes 5–8. Pronounce: Arduino (aar-DWEE-no), L298N (L-two-nine-eight-N), PWM (P-W-M), infrared (in-fra-red).

```text
# Video 9: Reading the IR sensors

[00:00:00.000] (Intro) MyCampusView Academy. Robotics 101, video 9. Reading the IR sensors.
[00:00:10.000] Before the robot can follow a line, we must be sure it can see the line. In this video, you will read both sensors and print what they see.
[00:00:21.500] First we give the pins names. LEFT_IR is pin 2 and RIGHT_IR is pin 3. ON_LINE says which value means black. For many sensors it is HIGH.
[00:00:36.000] digitalRead reads a pin and gives HIGH or LOW. Then we print BLACK or WHITE for each sensor. The short delay slows the printing down so you can read it.
[00:00:49.300] Upload the sketch and open the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud. Slide the robot slowly across the line. You should see the left sensor say BLACK, then the right one.
[00:01:04.200] If black shows as WHITE, your module is the other way round: change ON_LINE to LOW. If a sensor never changes, adjust its knob. If nothing prints, check the baud rate.
[00:01:18.900] (Outro) Let us recap. digitalRead() gives HIGH or LOW. The Serial Monitor shows what the robot sees. Set ON_LINE to match your sensors. Next: Controlling the motors in code.
```

[00:01:27.900] Now ask an adult to check four things: the battery wires are the right way round, no bare wires touch, every ground is connected, and the switch stays off until the check is done.
[00:01:41.200] (Outro) Let us recap. Sensors: OUT to D2 and D3. Driver: ENA D5, ENB D6, IN1–IN4 on D8–D11. All grounds connected together. Next: Your first Arduino program.

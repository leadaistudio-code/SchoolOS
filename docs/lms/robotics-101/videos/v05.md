# Video 5: How motors and the motor driver work

[00:00:00.000] (Intro) MyCampusView Academy. Robotics 101. How motors and the motor driver work.

[00:00:15.500] Inside the L298N are switches arranged like the letter H. That is called an H-bridge. Two control pins decide which way the current flows through the motor.
[00:00:30.000] If IN1 is HIGH and IN2 is LOW, the motor spins forward. Swap them, and it spins backward. If both are LOW, the motor stops.
[00:00:43.500] The enable pin sets the speed. The Arduino switches it on and off very fast. This is called PWM. On most of the time means fast. On only a little of the time means slow. Speed goes from 0 to 255.
[00:01:04.500] Our robot has no steering wheel. It turns by driving its wheels at different speeds. Both wheels the same speed: straight ahead. Left wheel slower: the robot curves left. Right wheel slower: it curves right.
[00:01:22.500] Watch the left and right motor numbers on the panel. When the line curves, one wheel slows down and the robot turns towards the line.
[00:01:37.500] (Outro) Next: Building the robot body.

```text
# Video 5: How motors and the motor driver work

[00:00:00.000] (Intro) MyCampusView Academy. Robotics 101, video 5. How motors and the motor driver work.
[00:00:10.700] Our robot has two motors, one for each wheel. Let us see how the Arduino controls their direction and speed.
[00:00:20.400] Inside the L298N are switches arranged like the letter H. That is called an H-bridge. Two control pins decide which way the current flows through the motor.
[00:00:33.000] If IN1 is HIGH and IN2 is LOW, the motor spins forward. Swap them, and it spins backward. If both are LOW, the motor stops.
[00:00:45.800] The enable pin sets the speed. The Arduino switches it on and off very fast. This is called PWM. On most of the time means fast. On only a little of the time means slow. Speed goes from 0 to 255.
[00:01:08.000] Our robot has no steering wheel. It turns by driving its wheels at different speeds. Both wheels the same speed: straight ahead. Left wheel slower: the robot curves left. Right wheel slower: it curves right.
[00:01:27.800] Watch the left and right motor numbers on the panel. When the line curves, one wheel slows down and the robot turns towards the line.
[00:01:42.800] (Outro) Let us recap. IN pins set direction. EN pins set speed with PWM. Different wheel speeds steer the robot. Next: Building the robot body.
```

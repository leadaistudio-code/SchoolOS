## Narration script

```text
# Video 2: Understanding robots: sense, think, act

[00:00:00.000] (Intro) MyCampusView Academy. Robotics 101. Understanding robots: sense, think, act.
[00:00:04.500] A fan spins and a toaster gets hot, but they are not robots. So what makes a machine a robot? Let us find out.
[00:00:17.500] Every robot does three things. First it senses: it collects information about the world. Then it thinks: a small computer decides what to do. Then it acts: it moves or makes something happen. And then it does it all again.
[00:00:38.000] You do this every day. When a friend throws you a ball, your eyes sense it, your brain thinks about where it will land, and your hands act to catch it.
[00:00:54.500] Our line-follower works the same way. Its infrared sensors sense the floor. The Arduino board thinks and chooses a direction. The motors act and turn the wheels.
[00:01:09.000] The robot repeats this loop very fast, hundreds of times every second. That is why it can react the moment the line bends.
[00:01:21.500] Try this. An automatic door senses you, decides, and opens, so it behaves like a robot. A remote-control car is different, because a person does the thinking for it.
[00:01:37.000] (Outro) Next: Exploring the hardware in your kit.
```

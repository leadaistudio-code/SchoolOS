## Short version (for email)

**MyCampusView** is one operating system for the whole school — Admissions CRM, Student Information System, and School ERP on a single connected record set.

**Subscription pricing (INR)**

| Plan | Yearly | Monthly | AI credits | Communication (SMS + WhatsApp) | Branded app |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter Pack** | **₹60,000 / year** | **₹5,500 / month** | 1,000 | 1,000 | — |
| **Growth Pack** *(recommended)* | **₹84,000 / year** | **₹7,500 / month** | 2,000 | 2,000 | Dedicated school-name app on **Play Store** |
| **Elite Pack** | **₹1,20,000 / year** | **₹1,26,000 / month** | 5,000 | 5,000 | Dedicated school-name app on **Play Store & App Store** |

GST, payment-gateway fees, excess SMS/WhatsApp credits, and any implementation fee are billed separately unless stated on the commercial schedule.

**Included for your school**

- **Academics** — classes, subjects, syllabus, timetable, homework, classwork, calendar  
- **Exams & certificates** — schedules, admit cards, marks, grading, results, report cards, bonafide / TC / character certificates  
- **Teaching tools** — question bank, paper builder, assessments & analytics; optional AI assist  
- **People & attendance** — student/staff records, daily & period attendance, leave, document vault, promotions, ID cards  
- **Admissions CRM** — enquiries, pipeline, follow-ups, conversion analytics, online form, front office / visitors  
- **Fees** — structures, invoices, collection & receipts, dues, concessions, expense tracker; online payments at setup  
- **Communication** — notices, messaging, email, templates; SMS / WhatsApp at setup; 360° feedback; Ask Me campus assistant  
- **Operations** — transport + live bus tracking, library, inventory, events, sports, school website  
- **Admin** — dashboard, reports, health score, roles & permissions, branding, audit trail, custom domain, PWA, MFA  
- **Access** — web portals for admin, teachers, accountant, parents & students; Android app  

**Typical go-live:** discovery → configuration → data migration → training → launch (~4 weeks, term boundary preferred).

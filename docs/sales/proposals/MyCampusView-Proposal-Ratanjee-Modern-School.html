Subject: Proposal to implement MyCampusView School ERP at Ratanjee Modern School

Dear Sir / Madam,

Thank you for the opportunity to present MyCampusView to Ratanjee Modern School. Most schools we meet run their day on a mix of registers, Excel sheets, separate accounting software and personal WhatsApp groups. Each works on its own, but together they cost the office hours every week, make fee follow-up slow, and leave management without one clear view of the school.

MyCampusView brings admissions, student records, attendance, academics, examinations, fees, accounts, parent communication, transport and campus operations onto a single, secure platform. Every module works from the same student record, so information is entered once and is available to the right people — the office, teachers, parents and management — instantly.

This proposal sets out the challenges we will solve, the scope of the solution for Ratanjee Modern School, the benefits for each group in your school, and a practical plan to go live within four to six weeks. Commercial terms will be shared separately in a formal quotation.

We would be glad to walk your team through a live demonstration using your own classes and fee structure.

Warm regards,Manmohan SinghGrowth Manager, MyCampusView
contact@mycampusview.com · www.mycampusview.com

Scope of the solution

Everything your school runs, on one platform

All modules share one student record and can be switched on in phases.

Admissions & front office

  • Enquiry pipeline with follow-ups and conversion reports
  • Online admission form that feeds the pipeline
  • Visitor log and appointments
  • Gate passes and student pickup with OTP / QR check

Student information

  • One record per child: profile, guardians, documents
  • Branded student and staff ID cards
  • Transfer certificates and year-end promotions
  • Bulk import of existing student data

Attendance & leave

  • Daily and period-wise registers on phone or desktop
  • Instant absence alerts to parents
  • Staff attendance with geofence or biometric devices
  • Leave apply → approve with balances

Academics & timetable

  • Automatic timetable generator with locked periods
  • Teacher substitution board with fair allocation
  • Homework, classwork log and syllabus tracking
  • Academic calendar for staff and parents

Examinations & report cards

  • Exam schedules, rooms and admit cards
  • Marks entry with review and approval workflow
  • Configurable grading and holistic report cards
  • Certificates with QR verification

Fees & collections

  • Fee structures, instalments and concessions
  • Counter, cheque and online collection with receipts
  • Live dues, reminders and late-fee rules
  • Day-end collection report for the accountant

Accounts & Tally

  • Chart of accounts and journal entries
  • Trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet
  • Expense heads and expense tracking
  • Export of fee and expense vouchers to Tally

Communication

  • Notices by class, section or role
  • Parent–teacher messaging inside the app
  • SMS and WhatsApp notifications
  • Daily WhatsApp summary for the principal

Parent self-service

  • Parent app: attendance, fees, results, homework
  • Online fee payment and receipt download
  • Two-way WhatsApp assistant for common queries
  • Parent queries routed to the front office

Transport

  • Buses, routes, stops and student assignment
  • Live bus tracking for the school and parents
  • Bus-approaching alerts to parents
  • Transport fees linked to the stop

Campus operations

  • Library catalogue, issue / return and fines
  • Inventory and school store with billing (POS)
  • Purchases, stock movements and sales reports
  • Events and sports management

CCTV live view

  • Principal and admins watch school cameras in the app
  • Optional classroom view for parents in set hours
  • Video is relayed live, never recorded by us
  • Simple relay app on a school PC; no router changes
Platform Web portals for management, office, accounts, teachers, parents and students · Android app · your school’s name, logo and colours on login, receipts, report cards and certificates · role-based permissions · two-factor sign-in · audit trail · data export at any time.

How we will implement

A typical plan for Ratanjee Modern School. Dates are fixed with your team at kick-off; we prefer to go live at the start of a term or month.

Discovery & setup
Kick-off with management, review of current registers and fee structure, school account and branding set up.
Weeks 1–2
Data migration
Students, parents, staff, classes, fee structures and opening dues imported from your existing sheets or software.
Weeks 2–3
Configuration
Fee plans and concessions, timetable, exam and report-card templates, roles and permissions, notifications.
Weeks 3–4
Training
Hands-on sessions for the office, accounts, teachers and front office, followed by a short parallel run.
Weeks 4–6
Go-live & parents
Parent app onboarding, first fee cycle and attendance on the system, close support during the first weeks.
